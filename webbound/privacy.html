Privacy Policy

WebBound Solitaire, by ParALImedia. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

This policy explains what information WebBound Solitaire (“the game”) uses on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux, and why. We do not ask for your name, email address or any account, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Your game progress

Your progress (score, campaign stars, diamonds, unlocked items, settings) is saved on your own device. We do not run servers that collect it.

  • Steam (Windows, macOS, Linux): the game uses your Steam account ID to keep a separate save per Steam account, to back the save up with Steam Cloud, to unlock Steam achievements and to post your total score to the Steam leaderboard (which shows your Steam name and avatar to other players). This is handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy.

2. Advertising (Android and iOS only)

The mobile versions are free and show ads through Google AdMob. The Steam versions show no ads.

  • To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google’s User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Rewarded ads (for example “Double (Ad)”) are always optional. Buying Remove Ads turns off the ads that show between games.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase (for example “Remove Ads”) was made, so it can be unlocked and restored.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your rights

Because your progress stays on your device, you can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the game. For advertising data, use the ad privacy options above or contact Google. For any other request about your data, write to us.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
